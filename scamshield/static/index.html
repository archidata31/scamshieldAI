        Safety guide

        The most common financial scams in India and how to respond.

        KYC / account-block

        “Update KYC or your account is blocked.” Banks do not send links to update KYC over SMS. Visit the branch or the official app.

        UPI collect & refund

        You never enter a UPI PIN to receive money. If a request asks for your PIN, it is taking money out.

        Prize, lottery & fees

        Genuine winnings never need a processing, tax or registration fee paid first.

        Digital arrest

        No agency arrests people over video call or asks you to stay on camera. Hang up and call 1930.

        Fake delivery & utility

        Messages threatening electricity, SIM or FASTag cut-off with a payment link are phishing. Check the provider's own app.

        Fake jobs & investments

        Daily income for liking videos and guaranteed returns are classic task-scam and Ponzi hooks.

        If you already clicked or paid

        1. Call your bank immediately and block cards, UPI and net banking.
        2. Call 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) within the first hour: faster reports improve the chance of freezing funds.
        3. File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in and keep screenshots of the message.
        4. Change passwords for any account whose details you shared.

        ScamShield provides a risk estimate, not proof. It can be wrong, so always confirm through your bank's official channels.
