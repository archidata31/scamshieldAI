        USER AWARENESS

        Financial Safety Guide

        Recognise common scams and know what to do when a message looks suspicious.

        STAY CYBER SAFE
        01

        KYC and account-block scams

        Messages threatening account suspension unless you update KYC through an unfamiliar link are common phishing attempts. Contact your bank using its official app or website.

        02

        UPI collect requests

        You do not need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Approving an unknown collect request can send money from your account.

        03

        Prize and lottery scams

        Unexpected prizes that require a registration, processing or tax fee upfront are a warning sign. Do not pay to claim an unsolicited prize.

        04

        Digital arrest threats

        No legitimate police or court authority arrests people over a video call or demands money to avoid arrest. End the call and verify independently.

        05

        Fake delivery and utility alerts

        Unexpected payment links claiming your electricity, SIM, FASTag or parcel service will be blocked should be checked through the provider's official app.

        06

        Fake jobs and investments

        Guaranteed returns, advance fees for jobs and daily income for simple online tasks can be signs of investment or task scams.

        !
        IF YOU ALREADY CLICKED OR PAID

        Act quickly. Protect your account.

        1. Call your bank immediately and block affected cards, UPI or net banking access.
        2. Call 1930, India's National Cyber Crime Helpline, as soon as possible.
        3. File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in and keep screenshots and transaction details.
        4. Change passwords for any account whose credentials you shared.

        ScamShield provides a risk estimate, not proof. Always verify through official channels.
